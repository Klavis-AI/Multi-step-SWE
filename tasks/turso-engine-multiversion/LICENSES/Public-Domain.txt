SQLite Is Public Domain

SQLite is in the Public Domain.

All of the code and documentation in SQLite has been dedicated to the public
domain by the authors.

All code authors, and representatives of the companies they work for, have
signed affidavits dedicating their contributions to the public domain and
originals of those signed affidavits are stored in a firesafe at the main
offices of Hwaci. All contributors are citizens of countries that allow
creative works to be dedicated into the public domain.

Anyone is free to copy, modify, publish, use, compile, sell, or distribute the
original SQLite code, either in source code form or as a compiled binary, for
any purpose, commercial or non-commercial, and by any means.

The previous paragraph applies to the deliverable code and documentation in
SQLite -- those parts of the SQLite library that you actually bundle and ship
with a larger application.

The author disclaims copyright to this source code. In place of a legal
notice, here is a blessing:

   May you do good and not evil.
   May you find forgiveness for yourself and forgive others.
   May you share freely, never taking more than you give.
