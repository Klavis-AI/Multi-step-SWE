0001-merge-difficulty-zero-is-accepted	EIP-3675 fixes the post-merge block difficulty at 0, so an env that states currentDifficulty = 0 is well-formed.
0002-merge-ommers-earn-nothing	EIP-3675 removes the ommer reward: the two ommer addresses are credited nothing and stay absent from the post-state.
0003-merge-empty-block-pays-no-one	EIP-3675 makes the block reward zero, so a post-merge block with no transactions leaves the coinbase uncreated.
0004-merge-coinbase-gets-the-tip-and-no-block-reward	EIP-3675 removes the block reward but not the EIP-1559 fee split: the coinbase ends with exactly tip*21000 = 189000 wei and no 2-ether subsidy on top.
0005-merge-ommers-do-not-change-the-coinbase-credit	Pre-merge, each included ommer added 1/32 of the block reward to the miner. EIP-3675 removes both, so the coinbase credit is the tip alone whether ommers are present or not.
