# 11 declarations, 4 forced, 7 open over 6 buckets in 5 files.

# WHAT open MEANS. A raw declaration whose type could carry arguments and does
# not. C-3's arithmetic is over these and only these.
#
# WHAT forced MEANS, and it is the denominator rather than decoration: the
# declaration cannot be narrowed without breaking something the tree does not
# own. All 4 here are parameters of methods implementing TestNG 5.14.6's
# IAnnotationTransformer, which declares them raw -- narrow them and the method
# stops overriding anything. The reference solution suppresses them and keeps
# them, which is why they also appear in suppress.txt's allow rows.
#
# FOUR DECLARATIONS SIT IN THREE BUCKETS ENDING IN '#-'. The declaration is a
# FIELD, and enclosingMethod() yields '-' outside a method, so two raw fields of
# one class collapse into one bucket -- which is why the four are three. That is
# the conservative direction: it shrinks the denominator rather than inflating
# it.
#

# RemoveDependsOnTransformer 38->39). The <file> and the in=<method> survive it;
# the <file>:<line> the same records carry does not, and is discarded here.
