Public-domain dedication for the Python Enhancement Proposals (PEPs)
bundled with python-package-resolver-multiversion
=====================================================================

Source: https://github.com/python/peps at commit
8766f78fee7b2bd08bce0537fcb2c9b103b10d71 (peps/pep-0001.rst, section 15,
"Copyright/license"). As rendered (the RST source marks
`CC0-1.0-Universal` as a hyperlink):

    Each new PEP must be placed under a dual license of
    public domain and CC0-1.0-Universal (see this PEP for an example).

The bundled PEPs carry one of the following statements. Each is quoted
verbatim from the PEP named beside it.

"This document has been placed in the public domain."

    pep-0425.rst
    pep-0440.rst
    pep-0503.rst
    pep-0508.rst
    pep-0517.rst
    pep-0518.rst
    pep-0592.rst

"This document has been placed into the public domain."

    pep-0427.rst

"This document is placed in the public domain or under the
CC0-1.0-Universal license, whichever is more permissive."

    pep-0621.rst
    pep-0631.rst
    pep-0656.rst
    pep-0658.rst
    pep-0660.rst
    pep-0691.rst
    pep-0714.rst
    pep-0723.rst
    pep-0730.rst
    pep-0735.rst
    pep-0751.rst

(pep-0730.rst wraps this statement after "CC0-1.0-Universal"; the wording is
otherwise identical.)

The set of bundled specifications grows from step to step; the union across
all fifteen steps is the 19 PEPs listed above plus `pep-0600.rst`, and 25 PyPA
Packaging User Guide pages. Each step's own MANIFEST.tsv records the documents
and licence for that step.

PEP 1 itself (not bundled as a specification) states, in its Copyright
section:

    This document is placed in the public domain or under the
    CC0-1.0-Universal license, whichever is more permissive.

pep-0600.rst contains no Copyright section; its public-domain or
CC0-1.0 status follows from the PEP 1 policy quoted above, as recorded
in MANIFEST.tsv ("public-domain OR CC0-1.0 (no Copyright section in
text; PEP 1 policy)").

The full CC0-1.0-Universal legal text is in LICENSES/CC0-1.0.txt.
