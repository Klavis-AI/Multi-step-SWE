The BoGo delta for this step is large and therefore coarse.

of the 1306 cases this step scores, the 0.23.31 shim -- run under this
step's own 0.23.45 harness and graded the loose-errors way this step
grades -- passes 925; the 381 it does not are this step's delta, frozen
here as the seven bogo-*-new groups. Across the full 2099-case BoGo suite
the same shim passes 1036, fails 415 and skips 648; every one of the 381
delta cases is among those 415 failures and every one of the 925 inherited
cases is among the 1036 it passes, checked case by case against the frozen
lists.

The delta is a valid discriminating set -- this step's Oracle passes
every case in it and the previous step's does not -- but it is decided
by a rustls-library change, not by a shim or harness migration. This
measurement holds the 0.23.45 harness fixed on both sides and varies
only the library under the shim, whose own call site is byte-identical
at both levels. 372 of the 381 fail the 0.23.31 shim with an empty-OCSP
decode error ("tls: error decoding CertificateStatus message" or
"... Certificate message"), and two more (RejectEmptyOCSPResponse-
TLS12/13) are the same mechanism seen from the peer: 0.23.31's
with_single_cert_with_ocsp sets certified_key.ocsp = Some(ocsp)
unconditionally and emits an empty CertificateStatus the newer test
client rejects, while 0.23.45 guards it with `if !ocsp.is_empty()`,
backed by PayloadU24<NonEmpty> in msgs/handshake.rs. The remaining
seven are further 0.23.31-to-0.23.45 library changes (four
cert-compression, three where the old shim drops the connection on a
key-share hint or legacy-connect case). None of this is the step's
headline behaviour (GHSA-2mjx-qc3c-rqvc, encryption-level alignment),
so this step also carries non-BoGo new-capability groups aimed directly
at that behaviour.
